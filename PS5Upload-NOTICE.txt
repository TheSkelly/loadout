Loadout sends games to the PS5 with PS5Upload.

ps5upload-engine.exe in this folder is the PS5Upload engine, version 6.1.0,
taken unchanged from the official PS5Upload release. It is a separate program
that Loadout starts in the background and talks to over a local connection.
