PS5 user profiles

Change the name and picture of the users on your PS5. Loadout does this through PS5Upload's helper, which it loads on the PS5 when needed. The PS5 may need a restart, or the user signed out and back in, before a change shows everywhere.

PS5 home screen

Hide tiles you don't want on the PS5's home screen, and bring them back later. Loadout reads the PS5's home screen list, keeps a backup copy on this PC, and flips only the "visible" switch for the tiles you pick.

This part is new and experimental. After hiding or showing a tile, restart the PS5 (a full restart, not rest mode) for it to take effect. If a tile comes back by itself, the PS5 has overruled the change; tell us which tile.

Payloads on the PS5

The payloads your PS5 loads after a jailbreak, with each one's version and whether a newer one is out. Loadout reads this from PS5 Payload Manager's folder, or from an autoloader folder if you use one of those.

Send a payload now

Send a payload file (.elf) from this PC straight to the PS5, like a payload sender app. Jailbreak the PS5 first so its ELF loader is listening (port 9021 with etaHEN and most setups).
